Draftia

One place for your team’s information.

Draftia is a desktop application designed to help League of Legends teams organize information used for team practice, match preparation, and draft planning.

View screenshots Product info

Independent third-party product. Not an official Riot Games product.

What Draftia does

Draftia is designed for premade teams, amateur competitive teams, and community tournament participants. It helps teams keep their information in one place before, during, and after scrims or matches.

Champion Pool

Organize each member’s champions by role, tags, and proficiency.

Matchup

Record manual synergy and counter notes for champion preparation.

BAN / PICK

Manually build and save draft scenarios for team discussion.

Schedule

Track scrims, tournaments, meetings, and team events.

Battle Note

Store opponent notes, match history, and prep information together.

Work

Keep feedback, improvement points, and next tasks visible for the team.

Product information

PlatformWindows desktop application
Data storageDraftia stores its data locally on the user’s PC.
Riot Web APIDraftia currently does not retrieve player or match data using the Riot Games Web API.
Gameplay automationNone. Draftia does not control the League of Legends client or automate in-game actions.
Decision makingStrategic decisions and draft choices remain manual and are made by players.
DistributionPublic distribution is planned after Riot registration and policy confirmation.
Monetization status
A paid version is under consideration. The final distribution and monetization model will be adjusted to comply with Riot Games policies before release.
