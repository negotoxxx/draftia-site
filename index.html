Draftia

One place for your team’s information.

Draftia helps League of Legends teams organize champion pools, matchup notes, draft planning, opponent research, schedules, feedback, and practice tasks.

View features View screenshots

Independent third-party product. Not an official Riot Games product.

What Draftia does

Draftia is designed for premade teams, amateur competitive teams, and community tournament participants.

Champion Pool

Organize each member’s champions by role, proficiency, tags, and flex roles.

Matchup

Record manual counter, synergy, and matchup notes for team preparation.

BAN / PICK

Manually build draft scenarios and review team composition needs.

Battle Note

Keep opponent, player, notable champion, and research notes together.

Schedule

Manage scrims, matches, and team practice schedules.

Work

Record feedback and tasks discovered during practice or scrims.

Product information

PlatformWindows desktop application
Data storageDraftia stores its application data locally on the user’s PC.
Riot Web APIDraftia currently does not retrieve player or match data using the Riot Games Web API.
Gameplay automationNone. Draftia does not control the League client or automate in-game actions.
Decision makingDraft planning and strategic decisions remain manual and are made by players.
DistributionPublic distribution is planned after Riot registration and policy confirmation.
Monetization status
A paid version is under consideration. The final distribution and monetization model will be adjusted to comply with Riot Games policies before release.
